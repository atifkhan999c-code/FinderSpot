Frequently Asked Questions

Common answers about reporting, claiming, and recovery policies

How long does the campus hold unclaimed property?

All found items turned in to the Central Lost & Found Office are securely logged and safely retained in our vaults for 90 calendar days. After 90 days, unclaimed clothing/books are donated to campus community charity, and electronic devices are recycled according to privacy data wiping standards.

How do I verify ownership if I don't have a purchase receipt?

You can verify ownership by unlocking a phone/laptop with your passcode, providing the lock screen image, stating identifying stickers or engravings, showing past photos with the item, or answering the confidential security question set by the founder.

Is there any fee or payment required to reclaim my item?

No. The campus lost-and-found service is 100% free of charge for all students, faculty, staff, and visitors. Finder rewards offered voluntarily by owners are managed directly between owners and finders.

What should I do if I find a student ID card or debit card?

Immediately report it on the portal under "Wallets & Cards" and bring the physical card to Room 104 or the nearest campus information desk. Staff will automatically send an internal campus notification to the student’s university email address.
