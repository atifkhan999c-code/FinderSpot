Our Purpose & Mission

Connecting People. Protecting Belongings. Restoring Peace of Mind.

FinderSpot is a digital lost-and-found management ecosystem engineered to bridge students, faculty, staff, and campus safety teams with rapid verification and ethical return workflows.

Anti-Fraud Verification

Our unique security challenge questions and serial verification prevent fraudulent claims, ensuring only genuine owners reclaim items.

Smart Match Alerts

Automated keyword and category matching scans the database constantly, immediately alerting victims when a matching item is registered.

Community Trust Network

Encouraging campus honesty and civic responsibility through verified user profiles, trust scores, and voluntary finder rewards.

Partnered with Campus Safety & Security

FinderSpot works directly alongside the Central Campus Security Desk in Building A, Room 104. High-value belongings such as laptops, wallets, and government IDs are kept in monitored custody vaults.
