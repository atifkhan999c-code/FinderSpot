How FinderSpot Operates

We bridge the gap between people who lost items and kind Samaritans or campus security who found them.

1

Publish a Report

Fill in detailed description, photos, location, and a private verification question within seconds.

2

Instant Smart Matching

Our system scans listings to discover matching reports based on category, date, and description keywords.

3

Verification & Claim

Owners answer confidential security questions or provide serial proof to eliminate false claims.

Safe Handover

Arrange pickup via designated campus security desks, front offices, or safe exchange zones.
